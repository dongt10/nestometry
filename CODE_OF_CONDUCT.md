# Community Conduct

This project expects respectful, evidence-focused collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Welcome corrections and distinguish disagreement from personal criticism.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, and source-use boundaries.
- Report uncertainty honestly rather than overstating a model's accuracy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Submitting occupied-room media, restricted material, or security-sensitive details without authorization.
- Knowingly misrepresenting sources, measurements, affiliations, or contributor identity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. For a confidential report, use the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and state that the report concerns community conduct. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
