## Preferred verification paths

1. A permitted official drawing that establishes the relevant room type and publication rights.
2. An authorized measurement of an unoccupied representative room, with date, method, uncertainty, and consent recorded.
3. Official vendor specifications for furniture only; these cannot verify the room shell.

If controlled drawings are obtained, do not commit them. Record only permitted, non-sensitive derived facts and retain any required safeguarding or disposal constraints outside the public repository.
