# Unit 3 Public-Source Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10.

These notes summarize the public evidence used by the checked-in Unit 3 room examples. They do not contain or redistribute source media.

## Primary sources

| Source | Use | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| [UC Berkeley Housing — Unit 3](https://housing.berkeley.edu/explore-housing-options/residence-halls/unit-3/) | Room-type descriptions, furnishings, published representative views, and the room-variation warning | No exact room dimensions are published |
| [Published double top view](https://housing.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/double_top-250px.jpg) | Canonical representative double layout | One published example; no numeric scale |
| [Official Unit 3 double tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aS5EwS9VjH4) | Documents a valid alternate double arrangement | A different room example, not a universal layout |
| [Official high-rise double example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O_PIZTMsdYA) | Furniture-profile and material analog | Not specific to Unit 3; medium-confidence analog only |
| [Facilities drawing request process](https://facilities.berkeley.edu/requesting-campus-building-drawings) | Potential path to verified dimensions | No drawing has been obtained or committed |

## Supported observations

- The published Unit 3 double example has a centered entry/window relationship, storage by the entry, beds along opposite side walls, and two center-aligned study stations accessible from opposite sides.
- The official Unit 3 double tour shows a different valid room example with desks at the window. The data record keeps that as a variation rather than blending both layouts.
- The official Unit 3 triple evidence supports a loft and bunk arrangement, three study stations, and built-in entry storage.
- UC Berkeley explicitly warns that representative room models can vary in size, layout, and furniture configuration.

## Dimensions

No checked official public source publishes numeric shell dimensions for these examples. Consequently:

- every exact `room_shell` axis remains `null` and `unknown`;
- `visualization_shell` values are explicitly estimated geometry inputs;
- object measurements are labeled estimated or unknown; and
- the accuracy tier remains `official_representative`.

The private working reference archive used for proportional analysis is not distributed and is not itself presented as a public source. Any future exact value must come from a permitted drawing or authorized measurement and must preserve privacy and building-security limits.

## Source-use boundaries

- Do not scrape or redistribute virtual-tour media.
- Do not commit source screenshots, copied textures, or imported meshes.
- Do not use occupied-room media or personal student information.
- Do not publish restricted drawings or building-security details.
- Treat publicly visible layouts as representative examples, not exact assigned rooms.
